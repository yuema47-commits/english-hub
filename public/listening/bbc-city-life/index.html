BBC — City Life Around the World

2026年07月22日 BBC 城市生活 听力笔记 难度：中级

Episode Overview

In this episode, two hosts discuss the pace of life in different cities around the world. They compare cities like Tokyo, London, New York, and smaller towns, exploring how urban environments affect our daily routines and mental health.

Key Vocabulary

  • hustle and bustle — the busy, noisy activity of a place
  • commute (v./n.) — to travel regularly between home and work
  • green spaces — parks and natural areas in a city
  • cost of living — the amount of money needed to cover basic expenses
  • work-life balance — the equilibrium between work and personal life

Listening Notes

Main Points

  1. Tokyo has one of the fastest-paced lifestyles — people walk quickly and trains are always packed.
  2. Londoners value their “quiet carriage” on the Underground — a cultural preference for silence during commutes.
  3. Smaller cities like Copenhagen prioritize cycling and green spaces, leading to higher happiness scores.

Expressions I Learned

  • “at a snail’s pace” — very slowly (opposite of city life!)
  • “round the clock” — all day and all night
  • “the concrete jungle” — a city with many buildings and few trees

Comprehension Check

The hosts concluded that while big cities offer more opportunities, the ideal living situation depends on individual personality. Some people thrive on energy and convenience; others need calm and nature.

My Thoughts

This episode made me think about what I value most in my daily environment. The phrase “concrete jungle” is vivid — it captures both the excitement and the exhaustion of city life.
