## About the Article

This reading note is based on a chapter from Joshua Becker's book about minimalism. The article discusses how simplifying our physical environment can lead to greater mental clarity and more meaningful experiences.

## Key Vocabulary

- **declutter** (v.) — to remove unnecessary items from a space
- **intentional** (adj.) — done on purpose; deliberate
- **mindful** (adj.) — conscious and aware of the present moment
- **accumulate** (v.) — to gather or collect over time

## Summary

The author argues that we often accumulate possessions not because we need them, but because we associate buying with happiness. However, research shows that experiences bring more lasting joy than material goods. By decluttering our homes, we create space for what truly matters — relationships, growth, and purpose.

## My Reflections

I found the concept of "intentional living" particularly inspiring. Instead of asking "Should I buy this?", I should ask "Does this add value to my life?" This simple shift in mindset can prevent a lot of unnecessary spending and clutter.

> "The less you own, the less you have to worry about."

## Useful Expressions

- "simplify your life" — 简化你的生活
- "let go of things" — 放下物品/执念
- "quality over quantity" — 质量胜于数量
